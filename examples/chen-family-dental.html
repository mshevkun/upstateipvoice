What she told the planner

  1. Dr. Maya Chen

    I’m opening my own family dental practice in Kingston — Chen Family Dental. Associate in Poughkeepsie for fourteen years. Broadway build-out, seeing patients the week of March 15, 2027. Six operatories. Those are treatment rooms, not phones.

  2. Architect

    A new dental office in Kingston, six rooms, still in build-out, opening mid-March. Phone counts stay planning figures.

  3. Dr. Maya Chen

    Two receptionists at the front, already hired. My husband is the office manager — billing, insurance, payroll. Clinical side is me, an associate joining in April, two hygienists, and two assistants. Patients should reach a person at the front desk first, not a menu.

  4. Architect

    Two at the front, one office manager, six clinicians, front desk picks up.

  5. Dr. Maya Chen

    Monday through Thursday 8 AM to 5 PM. Friday 8 to 2. Closed weekends. Lunch 12:30 to 1:30 Monday–Thursday — the desk is empty, phones should close. Friday is a short day, no lunch close.

  6. Architect

    Weekday phone hours with a Friday early close. Lunch is a scheduled close, not overnight voicemail.

  7. Dr. Maya Chen

    If both receptionists are with patients, take a message. They check it in the morning. They need two separate lines. If the front is slammed, ring my husband’s work mobile — never his personal cell, never in a recording.

  8. Architect

    Two independent front-desk identities. Work mobile can ring. Personal numbers stay off recordings.

  9. Dr. Maya Chen

    After hours, next-business-day voicemail. Front desk owns it. Swelling, trauma, or uncontrolled bleeding: try the on-call dentist. That’s only those cases, not every call. Rotate on-call with the associate. Hang up and call 911 for a true emergency.

  10. Architect

    Voicemail after hours. Urgent is staff-classified after someone answers. The published number does not jump to on-call by itself.

  11. Dr. Maya Chen

    One receptionist is bilingual. Spanish during open hours only — we cannot cover Spanish at 9 PM. Before we open, take a callback message and do not advertise the number until that recording exists. New number. Internet still needs a survey. Open Dental. Thanksgiving and Christmas closed. Snow days follow the closed-hours recording.

  12. Architect

    Spanish with staff during open hours. Before-opening callback is owned by the front desk. Cabling stays blocked until the space can be surveyed.
