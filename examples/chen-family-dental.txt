## Insights

CRITICAL Confirm your urgent-call handoff — After hours, callers press 9 to try the on-call dentist. During open hours, the front desk spots urgent calls after they answer. An on-call transfer is proposed but has not been tested on a real phone.
NOTE A work mobile can ring — Use a work app or Follow Me. A personal cell number is not stored and must not be spoken in any recording.
NOTE Lunch is a scheduled close — Phones follow the closed-hours path 12:30–13:30. That is not overnight voicemail, even if the recording is similar.
HIGH 6 clinical rooms do not automatically need 6 phones — Ask whether each room needs a direct line, intercom, emergency communication, or nothing. Do not order from room count.

## What a caller hears
During phone hours, if nobody answers: Thank you for calling Chen Family Dental. We are not able to take your call right now. Please leave your name, number, and reason for calling, and we will call you back.
After hours: You have reached Chen Family Dental after hours. If this is swelling, trauma, or uncontrolled bleeding, press 9 and we will try the on-call dentist. To leave a message, press 1 or wait. We return ordinary messages the next business day. If this is a life-threatening emergency, hang up and dial 911.
At lunch (12:30 PM–1:30 PM): Thank you for calling Chen Family Dental. We are closed for lunch and will return at 1:30 PM. Please leave your name, number, and reason for calling, and we will call you back this afternoon.
Before opening day: Thank you for calling Chen Family Dental. We are not yet open. Please leave your name, number, and a good time to reach you. We will call you back.
Press 9 after hours: Press 9 is part of your plan. It turns on once you give us a work on-call phone — not your personal cell.
If the desk is slammed: A work mobile can ring. We will not put a personal number in a recording.
